# Copy sweep: does any copy of a formula give a different answer from the engine?

Read-only sweep of OpenISD at `portingle/main` c60919f0 (18 Nov 2026). Nothing in the app or the allow-list was changed.
Method: for every allow-list group (`scripts/dupscan-allow.json`: 75 copy entries, 174 formula entries) that has a copy outside the engine
or two engine copies, a vitest script calls every copy and the engine's own function with the same inputs: 20 catalogue drivers
(rule in `scripts/copysweep/lib.ts`: every complete single-coil record in `packages/ui/public/drivers/`, sorted by path, index
floor(i*(n-1)/19)), 1/2/4 drivers, standard and isobaric, parallel and series wiring, Rg 0 and 1 ohm, coil heating 0 and 100 K, and three air states
where air matters. Tolerance 1e-9 relative unless the row says otherwise (sweep-derived numbers are read off a frequency grid, so those rows carry the stated tolerance).

Re-run: `npx vitest run -c scripts/copysweep/vitest.config.ts` (30 s), then `python3 scripts/copysweep/show.py` for the per-pair counts
(raw numbers in `scripts/copysweep/results/*.json`).
